1. Fetch / cascade justifications (fetch-strategy.md)
- Agency -> Vehicle: no cascade, vehicles survive agency deletion.
- Agency -> Employee: no cascade, employees are independent records.
- Vehicle <-> Equipment: no cascade, equipment is shared.
- Customer -> Reservation: PERSIST, saving a customer saves new reservations.
- Reservation -> Vehicle: no cascade, the vehicle exists independently.
- Reservation <-> Contract: ALL on Reservation side, a contract has no life without its reservation.
- Vehicle -> Maintenance: PERSIST, maintenance records are saved with the vehicle.

2. 401 vs 403
401: not authenticated (missing or wrong credentials).
403: authenticated but not allowed.


3. Authentication vs authorization
Authentication: proving who you are.
Authorization: deciding what you are allowed to do.
